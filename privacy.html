02Data Collection, Purpose & Where It Is Stored

Your privacy comes first. We never sell, rent, or share your personal or vehicle data with advertisers, insurers, data brokers, or authorities. We only process the minimal data needed to run the features you choose to use, and all sensitive account and garage data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256):

  • Account & Sign-In (Why & Where): Your email address and Google User ID are processed securely via Google Firebase Authentication solely to sign you in and protect your account.
  • Public Nickname ("Pseudo"): Shown only when you voluntarily share a fuel price update with the community. Note: To prevent abuse, the "Pseudo" cannot be modified once registered.
  • Optional Vehicle Garage & Service Reminders (Why & Where): If you choose to add your vehicle details (make, model, fuel type, tank size, consumption, odometer, and optional oil/tire/brake service intervals), they are used exclusively to calculate your personal fuel savings, range, and maintenance reminders. This data is stored locally on your phone and backed up in your private, encrypted Firebase Cloud Firestore vault (users/{uid}/vehicles, strictly isolated so only your authenticated account can read it) so you never lose your garage when switching phones.
  • Optional Fuel Logbook, Vehicle Expense Ledger & On-Device Tax/Financial Exports (Why & Where): Refuel entries (users/{uid}/refuel_logs) and vehicle expense ledger entries (users/{uid}/vehicle_expenses — maintenance, repairs, parts, insurance, cantonal road taxes, vignettes, leasing, parking/tolls, washing, fines, dates, odometer readings, and notes) are synced to your private encrypted cloud vault solely to compute your fuel economy, cost-per-kilometer (CHF/km), and expense history across your devices. Monthly/annual expense ledgers and cantonal tax reports (PDF/CSV) are generated 100% locally on your device only when you tap export, and are never shared with anyone unless you send the file yourself. They are permanently deleted when you delete the entry, vehicle, or account.
  • Foreground Location, Routing & Push Alerts (Why & Where): GPS coordinates are used only while the app is open on your screen to identify nearby stations, resolve city/address searches ("Teleport Me"), and calculate routes, and are never stored as a continuous location history on our servers. Foreground route distance and geocoding queries are processed anonymously via Project OSRM and OpenStreetMap Nominatim. If you enable favorite-station price-drop alerts or service notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push token and a coarse rounded region (~1.1 km grid) are stored in your encrypted profile to deliver relevant notifications.
  • Community Contributions: When you update a price, validate a price, vote on a promotional discount badge, or submit a station closure/issue report, your User ID is linked internally to prevent spam and maintain community data integrity.

We process your data based on:

  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR / Art. 31(2)(a) nFADP): To provide the app functions you requested.
