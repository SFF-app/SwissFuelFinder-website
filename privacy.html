02Data Collection, Purpose & Where It Is Stored

Your privacy comes first. We never sell, rent, or share your personal or vehicle data with advertisers, insurers, data brokers, or authorities. We only process the minimal data needed to run the features you choose to use, and all sensitive account and garage data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256):

  • Account & Sign-In (Why & Where): Your email address and Google User ID are processed securely via Google Firebase Authentication solely to sign you in and protect your account.
  • Public Nickname ("Pseudo"): Shown only when you voluntarily share a fuel price update with the community. Note: To prevent abuse, the "Pseudo" cannot be modified once registered.
  • Optional Vehicle Garage & Service Reminders (Why & Where): If you choose to add your vehicle details (make, model, fuel type, tank size, consumption, odometer, and optional oil/tire/brake service intervals), they are used exclusively to calculate your personal fuel savings, range, and maintenance reminders. This data is stored locally on your phone and backed up in your private, encrypted Firebase Cloud Firestore vault (strictly isolated so only your authenticated account can read it) so you never lose your garage when switching phones.
  • Optional Fuel Logbook & On-Device Tax Exports (Why & Where): Refuel entries you log (date, liters filled, cost in CHF, odometer) are synced to your private encrypted cloud vault solely to compute your fuel economy and expense history. Annual mileage and cantonal tax reports are generated 100% locally on your device only when you tap export, and are never shared with anyone unless you send the file yourself.
  • Foreground-Only Location (Why & Where): GPS coordinates are used only while the app is open on your screen to identify nearby stations and calculate routes. Your exact GPS coordinates are processed locally on your device and are never stored or tracked on our servers (only your general city/municipality name is saved in your encrypted profile to support regional station coverage).
  • Community Price Updates: When you update a price, your User ID is linked internally to prevent spam and maintain community data integrity.
03Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR / Art. 31(2)(a) nFADP): To provide the app functions you requested.
  • Legitimate Interests: To prevent fraud and improve app performance.
  • Consent: Specifically for personalized advertising (AdMob) and precise location tracking.
04Third-Party Services & International Data Transfer

To provide SFF, we use third-party providers. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside of Switzerland/EEA, notably the USA.

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: For infrastructure and database hosting.
  • Google AdMob: For ad delivery. You may opt-out of personalized tracking in your device settings.
  • Payrexx / TWINT: For payment processing. We do not store your credit card or bank details.
  • Safeguards: For transfers to the USA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
05Data Retention
  • Account Data: Retained as long as your account is active.
  • Technical Logs: Deleted after 30 days.
  • Legal Obligations: Payment-related data is stored for 10 years as required by the Swiss Code of Obligations.
06Your Rights

Under Swiss and EU law, you have the right to:

  • Information & Access: Request details on your stored data.
  • Correction: Update your vehicle profile and settings at any time. Please note that the Pseudo (Username) cannot be modified once chosen. If you wish to change your Pseudo, you must exercise your Right to Erasure to delete your account and create a new one.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a structured format.
  • Right to Object: Object to data processing for marketing purposes.
07Data Security & Encryption

We protect your private data using strict, industry-standard security measures: all communications are encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS, all cloud-stored account and vehicle data is encrypted at rest (AES-256), and cryptographic access rules ensure that only you can access your private garage and fuel logs.
