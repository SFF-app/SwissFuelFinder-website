01Data Controller

WTRH, Switzerland — info@swissfuelfinder.com
The Operator is the controller responsible for processing your personal data under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR.

02Data Collection, Purpose & Where It Is Stored

Your privacy comes first. We never sell, rent, or share your personal or vehicle data with advertisers, insurers, data brokers, or authorities. We only process the minimal data needed to run the features you choose to use, and all sensitive account and garage data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256):

  • Account & Sign-In (Why & Where): Your email address and Google ID are processed securely via Google Firebase Authentication solely to sign you in and protect your account.
  • Public Nickname ("Pseudo"): Shown only when you voluntarily share a fuel price update with the community. Note: To prevent abuse, your Pseudo cannot be changed once registered.
  • Optional Vehicle Garage & Service Reminders (Why & Where): If you choose to add your vehicle details (make, model, fuel type, tank size, consumption, odometer, and optional oil/tire/brake service intervals), they are used exclusively to calculate your personal fuel savings, range, and maintenance reminders. This data is stored locally on your phone and backed up in your private, encrypted Firebase Cloud Firestore vault (strictly isolated so only your authenticated account can read it) so you never lose your garage when switching phones.
  • Optional Fuel Logbook & On-Device Tax Exports (Why & Where): Refuel entries you log (date, liters, cost in CHF, odometer) are synced to your private encrypted cloud vault solely to compute your fuel economy and expense history. Annual mileage and cantonal tax reports are generated 100% locally on your device only when you tap export, and are never shared with anyone unless you send the file yourself.
  • Foreground-Only Location (Why & Where): Your GPS location is used only while the app is open on your screen to display nearby stations and calculate routes. Your exact GPS coordinates are processed on your device and are never stored or tracked on our servers (only your general city/municipality name is saved in your encrypted profile to support regional station coverage).
  • Community Price Updates: When you report a station price, your account ID is linked internally to prevent spam and keep community prices reliable.
03Legal Basis for Processing
  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR / Art. 31(2)(a) nFADP): To provide the app functions you requested.
  • Legitimate Interests: To prevent fraud and improve app performance.
  • Consent: For personalized advertising (AdMob) and precise location tracking.
04Third-Party Services & International Data Transfer

Your data may be transferred outside Switzerland/EEA, notably to the USA.

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: Infrastructure and database hosting.
  • Google AdMob: Ad delivery. Opt-out available in device settings.
  • Payrexx / TWINT: Payment processing. We do not store card or bank details.
  • Safeguards: Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the Swiss FDPIC.
05Data Retention
  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active.
  • Technical Logs: Deleted after 30 days.
  • Legal Obligations: Payment data stored for 10 years (Swiss Code of Obligations).
06Your Rights
  • Access: Request details on your stored data.
  • Correction: Update your profile (Pseudo cannot be changed — exercise Erasure right to reset).
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a structured format.
  • Right to Object: Object to data processing for marketing purposes.
07Data Security & Encryption

We protect your private data using strict, industry-standard security measures: all communications are encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS, all cloud-stored account and vehicle data is encrypted at rest (AES-256), and cryptographic access rules ensure that only you can access your private garage and fuel logs.
